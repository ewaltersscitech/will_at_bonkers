Velocity vs. time

What to look for

Horizontal: nothing pushes the ball sideways once it leaves the bat, so ax = 0 and the blue arrow is the same length at every snapshot: vx = v₀ cos θ.

Vertical: gravity pulls down the whole time, so vy = v₀ sin θ − g t. The orange arrow shrinks on the way up, is zero at the top, then grows pointing down. Equal time steps give equal changes in vy (9.8 m/s each second).

At the peak the ball is still moving: v = vx, not zero. Air resistance is ignored, and g = 9.8 m/s².
